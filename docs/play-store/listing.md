# Google Play listing

Name: kiln

Short description: Control your Claude, Codex and Pi sessions from your phone.

Full description:

Kiln brings the sessions running on your computer to your Android phone.

Pair with your computer to read conversations, send prompts, stop an agent turn and answer permission requests. Local Claude, Codex and Pi sessions run through ACP and share their status with kiln's terminal interface.

Group sessions by project, search titles, filter by agent or activity, and hide sessions without stopping them. Read-only cloud tasks open through verified provider links.

A labelled offline demo lets you explore sample sessions and permission controls before pairing.

Real sessions require kiln on a Linux computer, an ACP adapter for your chosen agent, and an HTTPS connection to the phone server. Tailscale Serve is one supported setup. Agent accounts and provider charges are separate.
