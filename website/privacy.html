kiln privacy policy

Updated 1 October 2026. This policy covers the kiln Android app, published by cjber.

Your machines and conversations

Kiln connects to the HTTPS machine you choose. Pairing sends a one-use invitation code and your phone model to that machine. The phone sends its pairing token with requests. Conversation messages, prompts, approval choices and session metadata pass between your phone and your selected machine. Your agent and cloud providers may process conversation content under their own policies.

The kiln maintainer does not operate a central conversation service or receive your conversations through the app. Your selected machine, network operator and providers control their own retention. HTTPS protects connections in transit; this is not a claim of end-to-end encryption across providers.

Local storage and removal

Machine addresses, names and pairing tokens are stored on your phone using Android Keystore encryption. Hidden-session preferences are stored locally. Android backup is disabled. Forget a machine to remove its saved credentials. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove local preferences. Revoke the phone with kiln revoke on the computer to disable its token. Removing a connection does not delete conversations stored on your computer or by a provider; use that provider's deletion controls.

QR scanning and Google services

Scanning uses Google's code scanner. Google says image processing occurs on the phone and it does not store scanned images or results. Its SDK collects device and application information, installation or device identifiers, feature events, performance metrics and errors for diagnostics and usage analytics. You can paste an invitation instead of scanning.

Kiln has no advertising, paid subscription, or maintainer-operated analytics service. Google SDK diagnostics are separate from conversation data.

Offline demo

The labelled offline demo uses samples on the phone. It does not connect to machines, invoke agents or save pairing credentials.

External links

Cloud tasks open verified provider links in a browser or another app. That service handles data under its own privacy policy.

Contact

Contact cjberragan@gmail.com about privacy. Do not include pairing tokens or private conversations in public GitHub issues.
